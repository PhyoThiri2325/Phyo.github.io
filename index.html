Pizza Sales Analysis Using SQL

In this project, I used SQL to explore and analyze pizza sales data. The analysis involved calculating key performance metrics such as total revenue, average order value, total pizzas sold, total orders, and average pizzas per order. I also examined order trends by day and month, sales distribution by pizza category and size, and identified the best and worst-selling pizzas based on revenue, quantity sold, and number of orders. The SQL queries provided insights into customer preferences, high-performing products, and seasonal sales patterns, forming the analytical foundation for the Power BI dashboard I created.
